Hi, I'm Mayank.

I build artificial intelligence systems that live entirely on the edge.

Currently, I'm an engineer at Google India, developing on-device AI for Pixel smartphones. My recent work includes co-designing and optimizing Gemma 4, and future iterations, to run efficiently within the strict hardware constraints of the Google Tensor SoC. Before joining Google, I was in the SF Bay Area building patented, cutting-edge computer vision pipelines for Verkada.

My journey into ML started at IIT Delhi and led me to Xerox Research Center India, where I spent two years developing computer vision solutions for transportation. To deepen my expertise, I earned an MS in Computer Vision from Carnegie Mellon University, researching under Prof. Jeff Schneider and building with the perception team at Uber ATG. Feel free to check out my publications and patents on Google Scholar, or explore some of my favorite projects below.
